One system runs on a Jetson. The other turns a question into a ranked list of places.

Thesis Aug to Dec 2025

Smart Cane with Audio-Visual Description

An on-device assistive system on an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano 8GB. A camera image goes to Qwen2.5-VL 3B through Ollama, then through offline English to Indonesian translation with ArgosTranslate, and out as Indonesian speech through Piper TTS.

  • Built the full path from camera to speech on the device.
  • Evaluated on 50 real images, each paired with 5 reference captions: 2 human, 3 LLM-generated.

Jetson Orin Nano 8GB, on-device

  1. Camera image
  2. Qwen2.5-VL 3B via Ollama
  3. ArgosTranslate, English to Indonesian
  4. Piper TTS, Indonesian speech
  • 0.45 Average METEOR 50 images, 5 references each
  • 24.1 s End-to-end latency Camera to speech
  • 25.5 W Peak power Jetson Orin Nano 8GB
  • 1.5 h Estimated runtime On a battery pack

Team project May to Jul 2025

TravelMate.AI

A semantic recommendation system for East Java destinations, hotels, and restaurants. It embeds the query and the listings with MiniLM and IndoBERT, ranks them by cosine similarity, and filters by location.

  • Scraped and cleaned a dataset of 90 hotels and 67 restaurants.
  • Fine-tuned MiniLM and tuned IndoBERT models with Keras Tuner in TensorFlow/Keras.
  • Worked in a team of 6 to connect the model to the backend and frontend.

Query to ranked places

  1. User question
  2. MiniLM and IndoBERT embeddings
  3. Cosine ranking with location filter
  4. Ranked destinations, hotels, restaurants
